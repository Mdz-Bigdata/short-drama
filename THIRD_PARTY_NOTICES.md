This project uses original, clean-room application code to implement interoperable short-drama production behavior. It does not vendor the reviewed AGPL, Elastic-2.0, unlicensed, or model-weight code. Before redistributing copied upstream assets or code, review the exact upstream revision and its current license.

Exact reviewed revisions, dates, capability IDs, license observations, and attribution strings are stored in `backend/app/data/upstream_sources.json` and rendered in the capability center.

The shot-transition rules (`backend/app/core/shot_transitions.py`, `backend/skills/shot-transitions-master/`) implement a rule summary the user supplied, 「多张分镜之间自然过渡的关系和规则」, restating standard film-editing grammar. Three WeChat articles were linked alongside it; they could not be retrieved (WeChat's verification page was returned) and no text or images from them are used or redistributed.

The style-pack engine (`backend/app/core/style_packs.py`, `prompt_budget.py`, `video_prompt_engine.py`) was written clean-room after reviewing three user-supplied works, none of which carries an open licence and none of which is vendored, quoted at length or redistributed here: the Seedance 2.5 film-look engine 「万物生漫剧场景复刻引擎 v3.0」 and the atom-punk engine 「Seedance 2.5 漫剧导演引擎 CausalChain Director Engine v1.0」 (both © their authors; the latter states personal study and creative use with attribution), and the commercial storyboard skill 「分镜脚本 Skill v2.1.0」 by 叩叩 / 飞驰影漫社 with its user manual. What is adapted from them is general film-production practice — a verbatim style lock plus a per-segment tone line, object-bound colour cards, named constraints, anti-leak rules for references drawn in another medium, tiered negative prompts, per-model prompt budgets with a truncation order, and continuity lock tags. The skill's own prose, knowledge-base files, scripts and web panel are not copied or executed, and its required vendor credit line is not reproduced in this project's output. Twelve further WeChat articles supplied with these files could not be retrieved (WeChat's verification page was returned) and nothing is attributed to them.
