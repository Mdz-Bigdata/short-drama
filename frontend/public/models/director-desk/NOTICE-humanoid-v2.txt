What changed: the motions were copied onto the v1 skeleton (the library's skeleton without its
fingertip and toe-tip bones), renamed, and stripped of channels that never leave the rest pose.
The mesh, the skin and the first ten motions are those of humanoid-v1.glb, byte for byte in
their data. Built by frontend/scripts/build-humanoid-motions.py; the library files themselves
are not included.
